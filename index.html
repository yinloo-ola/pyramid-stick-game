How to Play (Misère Nim)

1
Pyramid Setup:

16 sticks arranged in 4 rows (1, 3, 5, 7).

2
Your Turn:

Cross out 1, 2, or 3 sticks. All chosen sticks on a turn must be from the same horizontal row.

3
Misère Winning Rule:

The player left forced to take the very last stick loses the game! Leave your opponent with only 1 stick to claim victory.

4
Single Device:

Play with a friend by passing the phone between turns.
